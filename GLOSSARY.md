# HORTOS Glossary

This file will hold canonical system terminology. A term should be defined once and reused consistently across product, design, engineering, operations, and AI behavior.

## Current governance terms

### Accepted

A decision-record status indicating that the decision is authoritative within its stated scope.

### Authoritative document

An approved document that defines current requirements, meaning, principles, or behavior within a specified scope.

### Chief Systems Architect

The accountable role protecting system coherence, architectural consistency, documentation quality, decision traceability, and founder intent across HORTOS.

### Constitution

The complete governed body of HORTOS principles, decisions, domain definitions, specifications, and operating standards maintained in this repository.

### Constitutional document

A durable, system-wide document that governs multiple product or technical areas and should change slowly.

### Decision record

A numbered document that records the context, selected direction, alternatives, consequences, and reconsideration triggers for a durable decision.

### Domain owner

The role accountable for the correctness and maintenance of a defined product or technical domain.

### Specification

An approved definition of a domain, workflow, interface, data model, or expected system behavior.

### Working document

Research, proposals, experiments, or unresolved thinking that is not yet authoritative.
