## Decision entry format

Every active entry must include:

- a stable ID;
- the precise decision required;
- why it matters now;
- recommendation and alternatives;
- owner and approvers;
- deadline or blocking dependency;
- link to the resulting ADR once resolved.

Resolved entries must be removed from this active register and represented by an accepted ADR or approved specification.
